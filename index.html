Three originals. One story.

Each song stands alone. Together they trace an arc from first spark to the quiet, hard work of letting go.

Pockets Are Full

Traces the rush of young love, empty hands, and wide-open possibility. A reminder of how it felt before life got heavy, and why holding onto that spark still matters.
