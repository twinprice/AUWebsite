Acoustic Underground

Six musicians from Richmond, Virginia, playing covers and originals in three and four-part harmony since 2020.

See live dates Hear the originals

New dates go up on Bandsintown and Instagram first.

There's nothing better than surprising a room with songs it loves but doesn't hear covered often.

Acoustic Underground plays an eclectic mix of covers and originals. Our sound is built on three and four-part harmonies that give you goosebumps, rock-solid musicianship, and attention to detail you don't often find in the local music scene.

You'll catch us playing Suite: Judy Blue Eyes, In Your Eyes and Africa, alongside Noah Kahan's Stick Season and Rihanna's Umbrella. Some nights it's the entire second side of Abbey Road. Our originals fit right into the mix.

“Our only rules are play it well and make it fun. If you love live music and a great time while you listen, we're here for you.”

The music

Three originals, one story. Each song stands alone. Together they trace an arc from first spark to the quiet, hard work of letting go. All three were recorded with friends in a home studio.
