    Live in Richmond

    Six years of bars, breweries, and late-night harmonies.

    The band

    Six of us. Richmond roots. Built for everything from intimate listening rooms to full brewery stages.

    Acoustic Underground plays an eclectic mix of covers and originals. For us, there's nothing better than surprising an audience with songs they love but don't hear covered often.

    Our sound is defined by three and four-part harmonies that give you goosebumps, rock-solid musicianship, and attention to detail that you don't often find in the local music scene.

    You'll catch us playing favorites like Suite: Judy Blue Eyes, In Your Eyes, and The Chain, alongside Noah Kahan's Stick Season and Rihanna's Umbrella. You might even catch Pink Floyd's Brain Damage into Eclipse. And our originals fit right into the mix.

    "Our only rules are play it well and make it fun. If you love live music and having a great time while you listen, we're here for you."

    Don Busick
