Round 4: six band icons

Each icon below is a real detachable mark. It lives next to the wordmark in full lockup, but also works alone on a sticker, a pick, a pin, or a social avatar. Each concept pulls from a different part of the band's identity: the word "underground," the word "acoustic," harmonies, Richmond, and two that fuse ideas together.

16

The Roots mark

Concept: underground · growth · foundation

A tree's root system below a horizon line. The word "underground" made literal. Three main roots reference your three-and-four-part harmonies. Graphic, woodcut, reads at every size. Feels folk without being a guitar-shaped cliché.

Sound hole & strings

Concept: acoustic · resonance

A guitar sound hole viewed straight on, with six strings crossing it and one string breaking into a sound wave. Immediately readable as "acoustic" without drawing a whole guitar. The broken string wiggling into a waveform is the twist that makes it yours instead of generic.

Harmony stack

Concept: three & four-part harmonies

Four horizontal waveforms stacked, each one slightly offset from the next, representing the four voices in the band's harmonies. Your single biggest differentiator rendered as a mark. Clean, abstract, distinctive. The top wave in amber is the lead vocal.

James River bridge

Concept: Richmond · local signature

A silhouette of the Lee Bridge crossing the James River, with the moon above. Every Richmonder knows this view. Ties the band to a specific place the way Rolling Stones have London or Allman Brothers have Georgia. Local pride without being a state flag or an RVA monogram.

Needle drop

Concept: the moment music begins

A turntable needle coming down onto a record, seen in profile. The tonearm crosses at a diagonal, the record is a single thick circle, and grooves radiate in two thin rings. Universally read as "music about to happen." Gives you the same hardware-as-icon feeling AC/DC gets from their lightning bolt or Iron Maiden gets from Eddie.

The Underground — tunnel/cave with an AU at the vanishing point

Concept: underground · literal depth

Three concentric arches receding into darkness, like looking down a tunnel or into a cave. An "AU" monogram glows at the vanishing point. Most literal rendering of "underground," but the concentric arches give it a distinctive, architectural feel rather than a cartoon cave. This echoes the "Cave Mouth" exploration from your earlier design work but resolves it as a proper icon.

Where we are: six icons, each with a genuine concept behind it. Each works as a detachable mark (middle variant column) and as a full lockup with the wordmark.

A real question: do any of these make you want to put them on a t-shirt? That's the right test for a band icon, not "is it clever," not "does it match the brand," but "would a fan wear this?" If the answer is no on all six, tell me which concepts (roots, sound hole, harmony, bridge, needle drop, tunnel) feel closest to right and I'll rebuild just that one with better execution.

Also: the wordmark typography is currently Playfair italic over Archivo Black. That's placeholder. Whatever direction wins, we lock the wordmark separately.
