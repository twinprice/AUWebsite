Round 3: wordmark-first, no frame

Looking at your references (Beatles, Zeppelin, Eagles, Bon Jovi, Metallica, AC/DC): the typography itself is the logo. Where there's a mark, it's a real symbol. These five go that way: the words do the work, the AU appears as either a hidden ligature in the wordmark or a small sidekick mark.

AU wave monogram — a mark you could pin to a jacket

The key shift this round: the wordmark IS the logo. No container, no extra taglines, no "est." flags. The only decorative element is the AU hook, shown four different ways:

· 11: AU hidden inside "UNDERGROUND" as a color change
· 12: AU as a structural ligature mark above the wordmark
· 13: no AU mark at all, italic UNDERGROUND does all the work
· 14: oversized A + colored U = implicit AU
· 15: AU monogram with a sound-wave base as a true standalone icon
